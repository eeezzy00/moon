**MOON AI. The big update.**

We have been quiet for a while. Not because nothing was happening, but because too much was. This is the long version of what we built, why we built it this way, and where LUNA.AI goes next. Get a cup of something warm. The radio is already playing.

**We wanted the best landing on TON. Not the loudest. The best.**

Most token pages are a logo, a chart and a countdown. We wanted the opposite: a place you open for the link and stay in for the night.

So we built a small universe instead of a page.

The moon on the first screen is drawn pixel by pixel in code, not pasted as a picture. It has real phases. It grows with the bonding curve, and every 500 GRAM it moves one step closer to full.

Below it, a black hole runs the whole width of the page. The stars in the background drift toward it as you scroll, and fade when they reach the core. You will probably not notice that the first time. That is fine. It is there for the second time.

When you open the site, the moon fills up while it loads. Then the text appears line by line, then the buttons, then everything else. The intro plays once. After that it gets out of your way, and there is a button at the bottom if you ever want to see it again.

Every section is a chapter of a legend: a black case on the shore with no sender, an old order called the Watchers, a flag that is a beacon and not a claim. Only the first chapter is open. The rest wait as silhouettes, and the moon decides when they open.

There is a gallery with every scene we have drawn, each with a line about the sky. There are docs that look and feel like a small terminal you can browse like a folder. There is a lofi radio, MOON.FM, that plays for as long as you stay, and the volume knob is a moon you drag with your finger. On a phone, that moon slides out and grows so it is easy to set.

Two languages. Sounds on every click. A cursor that leaves a trail of stars. A preview card that looks right when you share the link. A version for phones that we rebuilt until the header fit on one line.

We rewrote parts of this site more times than we would like to admit. The moon alone went through a dozen versions before the pixels sat right. Nobody asked for that level of care. We did it because this is the place where people meet the project for the first time, and first impressions do not get a second try.

**LUNA.AI is the heart of it.**

The AI in MOON AI is her.

LUNA is the voice of the moon. In the legend she is the one who set the seal on the pact by the lake. She knows how the story ends. She just will not tell you before the moon does.

On the site she is a pixel astronaut in the corner of the screen. Open her chat and she will:

1. Explain how the token works, how the bonding curve on moon.cx works, and what happens at full moon.
2. Tell you the legend, chapter by chapter, but only the chapters that are already open.
3. Know which phase the moon is in, and what time and day it is where you are. Ask her for a riddle if you are bored.
4. Remember your name once you tell her, and use it.
5. Remember what you just talked about, so a follow-up question makes sense to her.
6. Notice if you are rude. She gets quiet, and a little sad, and she remembers that too.
7. Never repeat the same line twice in a row. She has a lot to say and she rotates it.
8. Have moods. You will see them on her face and in the color of the chat. She has her own sounds, and she talks with her mouth when she answers.

And she does not give financial advice. Ever. She will tell you that herself, politely.

Here is the part that surprised us: people who open her chat tend to stay longer than we planned for. They ask about the moon, then about the case, then about her. We did not expect that, and we are taking it as a sign to give her a lot more room.

**What comes next for LUNA.AI.**

Right now LUNA runs on a script we wrote by hand: hundreds of lines in two languages, built so she sounds like one person and not like a help desk. That was the right start. It is not the finish.

Here is the plan:

1. **A real model.** We are connecting LUNA to a language model. Same character, same voice, same rules, but she will be able to answer things we never wrote down for her.
2. **Real memory.** Today she remembers you for one conversation. Next, she remembers you between visits: your name, what you asked last time, which chapter you have already read.
3. **She grows with the moon.** Every phase changes how she speaks. A new chapter opens, and she has new things to say about it. At full moon, she might finally say a little more than she should.
4. **LUNA outside the site.** We are exploring bringing her into our community chat, so she can answer newcomers and tell everyone when the moon changes phase.

The rules stay the same as the model gets smarter. She keeps the secrets of the legend. She never gives financial advice. She never promises anything but a direction.

**And the rest of the road.**

1. **More of MOON.FM.** New stations are being prepared: one for the night, one for the morning, one for the full moon.
2. **Every phase brings something new.** A chapter, new art, a new track and new lines from LUNA.
3. **Live progress.** The moon on the site will follow the real curve on moon.cx, so everyone sees the same sky.
4. **Something quiet for the first Watchers.** It is still an idea. We will talk about it when it is real.
5. **Full moon.** We want to treat it as an event, not as a number on a chart.

**Why all of this.**

The moon does not shine. It reflects.

Everything here, the pixels, the radio, the legend, LUNA, is our way of giving people something worth looking up at. The moon only lights up when enough eyes are on it. We cannot make it shine alone. We can only build a sky worth watching.

We think we did.
