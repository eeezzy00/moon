## Вариант A. Длинный пост (X Premium)

Update 1.1 from the shore

It has been a quiet few weeks on the outside. On the inside, a lot has moved.

The moon is alive.
When we started, MOON AI was one pixel moon on a black screen. Today it is a small universe. A moon that grows with the curve. A black hole at the edge of the page that pulls the stars in. Every phase has its own chapter of the legend, and each chapter stays a silhouette until the moon is ready for it. We rebuilt it more than once, because it never felt like enough. Now it does.

LUNA.AI became the heart of it.
She started as a small guide, a pixel astronaut who answers questions. She grew into the voice of the moon. She has moods and sounds, she knows the phase, the rules, the contracts and the story so far. People who open the chat tend to stay longer than we planned for. We did not expect that, and we are taking it as a sign to give her more room. She does not give financial advice, and she will tell you so herself.

There is a radio now.
MOON.FM plays soft lofi for as long as you stay. The volume knob is a moon you drag with your finger. New stations are being prepared: one for the night, one for the morning, one for the full moon.

A gallery of everything we have drawn.
Quiet rooms, a lamp, a window, a cat that watches the sky. Every picture has one line about the stars under it.

And a hundred small things.
The site works properly on phones. The docs are a small terminal you can browse like a folder. The intro plays once and then gets out of your way. Nobody will ever notice most of this. We did it anyway.

Where we are heading:
1. A deeper LUNA.AI, with a memory of the legend and a different voice in every chapter.
2. More of MOON.FM, a sound for every phase.
3. Every phase brings something new: a chapter, new art, a new track, a new line from LUNA.
4. Live progress on the site that follows the real curve on moon.cx.
5. Something quiet for the first Watchers. Still an idea. We will talk about it when it is real.
6. Full moon as an event, not a number on a chart.

No promises about price or timing. Only a direction.
Light is in no hurry, but it always arrives.

The moon is waiting at moonai.wiki
Early access in Telegram.

Nobody walks in the dark alone.

NFA. DYOR.
